# Project PRD: Road Infrastructure & Transparency Portal

## Project Overview
A professional, government-standard digital platform designed to provide citizens with real-time transparency into road infrastructure quality, maintenance schedules, and budget allocations. The portal empowers users to report issues (like potholes) and track their resolution through an AI-enhanced interface.

## Target Audience
- **General Citizens:** Reporting road issues and checking local road conditions.
- **Government Officials:** Monitoring network-wide quality metrics and contractor performance.
- **Data Analysts/Journalists:** Accessing public datasets for infrastructure accountability.

## Key Features

### 1. Immersive Home Portal
- **Visual Identity:** Modern, high-fidelity government aesthetic with immersive hero imagery (road-focused).
- **Core Navigation:** Quick access to "File a Complaint," "Browse Road Data," and "Live Quality Feed."
- **Transparency Metrics:** High-level dashboard stats showing resolved issues and KM surveyed.

### 2. Smart Road Complaint System
- **Interactive Mapping:** Geographic pinning of issues using a high-detail map interface.
- **AI-Assisted Filing:** An integrated AI chatbot to guide users through the reporting process.
- **Evidence Upload:** Support for image uploads with verified tracking and 24h evaluation SLAs.

### 3. Public Road Data Dashboard
- **Infrastructure Inventory:** Searchable and filterable database of road IDs, contractors, and budget status.
- **Quality Scoring:** Transparency on maintenance history and current health scores (0-100).
- **Geospatial Analysis:** Heatmaps and density links for infrastructure health across regions.

### 4. Live Quality Monitoring
- **IoT Integration:** Real-time vibration and accelerometer data for pothole detection.
- **Environmental Sensing:** Monitoring of road surface temperatures and moisture levels.
- **System Intelligence Workflow:** Automated detection, GPS tagging, and quality updates.

## Technical & Design Requirements
- **Design System:** "Civic Modern" (Inter typography, #0f4c81 primary blue, 8px roundness).
- **Interactivity:** Subtle motion effects (parallax, shimmers) to denote a "living" data environment.
- **Accessibility:** High-contrast text, standard government headers, and clear hierarchical layout.
- **Persistent AI Support:** Floating assistant available on every screen for contextual help.

## User Flows
1. **Reporting:** Citizen pins location -> AI assists with details -> Receipt generated.
2. **Monitoring:** User opens Live Feed -> Filters by region -> Observes real-time sensor spikes.
3. **Audit:** Journalist searches Road ID -> Reviews contractor history -> Downloads PDF audit report.
